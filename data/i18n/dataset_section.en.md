### PDF Materials
The PDF files contain a list of kanji characters and their associated vocabulary.
{pdfs}

### ANKI Decks
The decks can be re-imported into the ANKI application. They are placed under the 'KanTanJi'
collection and enable smart, interactive kanji practice. Each deck contains both kanji (recognisable
by the question card linking to KanjiAlive) and the related vocabulary.
Tap or click a card to reveal the furigana.

Every set comes in two variants: **basic** (kanji + essential vocabulary) and **extended**
(advanced vocabulary). We recommend starting with the basic deck and adding the extended one
to the same collection later.

{ankis}

### HTML
The HTML pages are intended for embedding interactive Kanji information in external web services.
{htmls}

### Data Packs
For importing into other applications, for example [Lively Wallpaper](https://github.com/KanjiBase/LivelyKanji).
